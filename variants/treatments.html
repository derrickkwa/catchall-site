Five visual treatments

Same layout, same copy, same structure. Only type, colour and illustration language change. Each slice shows a hero, the three-column gap block, the loop as it would be drawn, and a type specimen.

A · Ink & Brass

current

Ink navy, slate and brass. Instrument Sans with JetBrains Mono. Illustration is a generative field of completing cells. Confident and technical; the risk is that dark-plus-accent is the current house style of the whole category.

The work is getting automated.
The judgment isn’t.

AI today makes a formidable intern. What’s missing is everything an executive does.
